FAQ

What is this app?

A small Vite + React notes app that was broken on purpose with five problems often seen in AI-built apps, then diagnosed and fixed one commit at a time.

Which five problems were fixed?

A TypeScript build failure, an env var missing the VITE_ prefix, an auth redirect loop, a Supabase RLS policy that blocked reads, and a stray 404.html that broke SPA routing on Cloudflare Pages.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Any email signs in, nothing is sent, and notes stay in your browser's localStorage.
